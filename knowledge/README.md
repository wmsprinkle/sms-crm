# Product knowledge folder

Drop your life & health insurance product documents here as .md, .txt,
or .pdf (PDFs need: pip install pypdf).

Everything in this folder becomes the ONLY product facts the agent is
allowed to use. If the answer isn't in here, the agent says the licensed
agent will cover it on the call — it will not guess.

Good things to include:
- One file per product line (term life, whole life, health plans, etc.)
- Plain-language descriptions: who it's for, what it covers, term lengths
- FAQ answers you already give leads
- What the intro call covers and how long it takes

Do NOT include: rate sheets you don't want quoted, internal notes,
anything you wouldn't want repeated verbatim to a lead.
