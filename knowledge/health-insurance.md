# Health Insurance Plans

## Coverage Types
We offer individual and family health insurance plans designed for flexibility and affordability.

### Plan Levels
- **Bronze**: Lower premiums, higher out-of-pocket; good for healthy individuals
- **Silver**: Balanced premiums and out-of-pocket costs; most popular choice
- **Gold**: Higher premiums, lower out-of-pocket; best for frequent medical needs
- **Platinum**: Comprehensive coverage with minimal out-of-pocket costs

## Key Features
- **Preventive care**: Annual wellness exam, screenings, and vaccinations covered at no cost
- **Prescription coverage**: Tiered copay structure for medications
- **Network doctors**: Access to broad network of in-network providers
- **Deductible options**: Choose the deductible level that fits your budget
- **Out-of-pocket maximum**: Annual cap protects you from catastrophic costs

## Special Situations
- **Pre-existing conditions**: Cannot be excluded or penalized
- **Pregnancy**: Maternity coverage included on all plans
- **Dependents**: Children can stay on plan until age 26
- **Subsidy eligibility**: May qualify for federal tax credits to lower premiums

## Enrollment
- Open enrollment typically November-December for January coverage
- Special enrollment available after major life events (marriage, birth, job loss)
- Effective dates vary based on when application is submitted

## Support
Licensed agents can discuss plan options that match your family's health needs and budget, answer questions about coverage, and help determine if you qualify for subsidies.
