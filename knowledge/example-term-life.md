# Term Life Insurance

## Overview
We offer affordable, straightforward term life policies that provide income replacement during the years your family needs it most.

## Term Options
- **10-year term**: Protection for early phase of career/family growth
- **20-year term**: Standard coverage through peak earning/family years  
- **30-year term**: Extended protection to near-retirement

## Coverage Amounts
Available from $100,000 to $2,000,000 in increments, tailored to your income replacement needs.

## Key Benefits
- **Level premiums**: Your rate stays the same for the full term — no surprises
- **Simple application**: Many applicants approved without a medical exam (underwriting approval confirmed on call)
- **Conversion options**: Many policies allow conversion to permanent coverage later if needs change
- **No cash value**: Lower premiums because you're paying for protection only, not investment features

## Who It's For
- Protecting a mortgage or major debt
- Income replacement for family dependents
- Education funding for children
- Business continuation (if owner)
- Income gap coverage during peak working years

## Common Questions
- **How fast can I get coverage?** Licensed agent discusses eligibility on the call; many applicants are approved within days
- **What if my health changes later?** Policy continues regardless; coverage locks in at application
- **Can I increase coverage?** Term policies are typically level; conversion to permanent coverage may offer adjustments

## Next Steps
A licensed agent will walk through your specific situation, confirm eligibility, and answer any coverage or rate questions on a quick call.
